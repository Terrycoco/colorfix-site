ColorFix by Terry Privacy Policy

ColorFix by Terry is a personal color and design website operated by Terry Marr.

Information we collect:

When visitors use ColorFix, we may collect basic information such as page views, playlist views, referral/source information, and contact form submissions. If a visitor submits a message through the site, we may collect the name, email address, and message they provide.

How we use information:

We use this information to respond to messages, improve the website, understand which playlists and pages are being viewed, and create a better ColorFix experience.

Pinterest publishing:

ColorFix may use Pinterest's API to publish pins from generated ColorFix images to Terry Marr's Pinterest account. ColorFix does not sell Pinterest data or visitor data.

Cookies and analytics:

ColorFix may use basic cookies or analytics-style tracking to measure site usage and playlist engagement.

Data sharing:

ColorFix does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting providers, analytics tools, or connected services necessary to operate the website.

Contact:

For privacy questions, contact Terry Marr through the ColorFix contact form or by email.
