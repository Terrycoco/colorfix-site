ColorFix by Terry Privacy Policy

ColorFix by Terry is a personal color and design website operated by Terry Marr.

Information We Collect

When visitors use ColorFix, we may collect basic usage information, including page views, playlist views, referral/source information, and playlist engagement activity.

If a visitor submits a message through the site, we may collect the name, email address, and message they provide.

How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to visitor messages
  • Improve the website
  • Understand which playlists, pages, and features are being viewed
  • Create a better ColorFix experience
  • Track the performance of shared links, playlists, and published content

Pinterest Publishing

ColorFix may use Pinterest's API to publish Pins from generated ColorFix images and related ColorFix content to Terry Marr's Pinterest account.

ColorFix does not scrape Pinterest, post on behalf of other Pinterest users, or sell Pinterest data. Any Pinterest API access is used only to connect ColorFix content with Terry Marr's Pinterest account and publish original ColorFix materials.

Cookies and Analytics

ColorFix may use basic cookies, server logs, or analytics-style tracking to measure site usage, referral sources, playlist views, and engagement.

Data Sharing

ColorFix does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by hosting providers, analytics tools, or connected services necessary to operate the website, publish content, maintain the site, or respond to visitor messages.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Terry Marr through the ColorFix contact form or by email at terry@terrymarr.com.
